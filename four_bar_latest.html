How the web version computes the mechanism

For each θ, the endpoint of L1 is computed first. The shared joint between L2 and L3 is then found from the exact intersection of two circles: one of radius L2 about that endpoint and one of radius L3 about the right ground pivot. φ is obtained directly from that physical joint with atan2.
